ShetkariSetu

Useful farming information, made easier to find. A farmer-support platform concept that organises farming resources in one accessible place, with a focus on clear information and simple navigation.

Problem statement

Farmers may need to look in different places for crop guidance, weather updates, market information, and practical educational resources.

Proposed solution

A simple dashboard groups resources into clear categories. Any live agricultural data would need trusted sources before launch.

Project overview

A farmer-support platform concept that organises farming resources in one accessible place, with a focus on clear information and simple navigation.

HTMLCSSJavaScriptResponsive UIExternal data APIs (planned)

Typical user flow

  1. Choose crop guidance, weather, or market updates.
  2. Select a crop or location when needed.
  3. Read the information with its source and last-updated time.
  4. Open related guides or return to the dashboard.

Future development

  • Validate content with agricultural experts and intended users.
  • Integrate trusted weather and market-data APIs.
  • Add Marathi language support and accessible typography.
  • Display data freshness, sources, and disclaimers.
Features & experience

What the project is designed to include

Crop guidance

A content area for crop-specific tips and seasonal checklists.

Weather information

A planned weather panel connected to a reliable weather provider.

Market updates

A planned market-price area that displays its source and update date.

Learning resources

Guides and FAQs written in accessible language.

Local-language readiness

A design direction that could support Marathi and other regional languages.

Simple navigation

Clear sections intended to be easy to use on mobile devices.
